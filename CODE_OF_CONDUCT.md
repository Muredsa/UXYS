# Code of Conduct

UXYS welcomes discussion, critique, experiments, and competing interpretations of UX problems.

Contributors and participants are expected to:

- critique ideas, methods, and evidence rather than people;
- be specific about disagreements and provide reproducible examples where possible;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks;
- respect privacy and do not post private customer pages, recordings, analytics, credentials, or source code without authorization;
- distinguish measured evidence from model predictions;
- assume good faith while remaining rigorous about claims.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

For security-sensitive matters, follow `SECURITY.md` rather than opening a public issue.
